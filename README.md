# Cube Lens Community

Welcome to the public issue tracker for **Cube Lens**, the Android app by
[Cubiq Minds](https://www.cubiqminds.com/) that scans your magic
cube with the camera and guides you through the solution step by step.

**Install Cube Lens on Android:**
[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cubiqminds.cubelens&referrer=utm_source=github)

## What To Use This Repo For

Please use [Issues](https://github.com/cubiqminds/cubelens-community/issues) to
report bugs, request improvements, or share feedback about the Cube Lens Android
app. The app is designed for fast cube scanning, AI-assisted solving,
beginner-friendly instructions, and visual guidance through AR or a 3D cube view.

## Helpful Links

- [Open a new issue](https://github.com/cubiqminds/cubelens-community/issues/new/choose)
- [Download Cube Lens on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cubiqminds.cubelens&referrer=utm_source=github)
- [Visit the Cube Lens website](https://www.cubiqminds.com/)

## Before Opening An Issue

Please include your Android version, device model, Cube Lens app version, what
you expected to happen, and what actually happened. Screenshots or screen
recordings are especially helpful for scanning, AR guidance, or solving issues.
